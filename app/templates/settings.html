Shipping / Logistics

Set your shipping contract rates. Inbound is charged only on materials you need to buy (owned materials are already on-site). Outbound is charged on finished goods shipped back to market.

Market Fees

Profitability assumes instant transactions — materials bought from sell orders, finished goods sold to buy orders. No broker fee is charged (no sell order is placed).

The system where you install manufacturing and reaction jobs. Used to look up the system cost index — the main variable component of job installation fees. Sync your characters to populate cost index data.
